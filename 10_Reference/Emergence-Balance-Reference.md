# Reference — Emergence from the Balance Differential

> **Structural Causal Chain (LIMEN):**
> perfect symmetry (timeless, directionless) → constraint accumulation → overflow (A2) → **the balance differential ΔB** → emergence cascade: scalar → vector → tensor → declarative components → dynamics — with energy as the fundamental expression and measurement as self-referential.

This note is the **family reference** for the emergence doctrine: what "emergence" means in the corpus, what issues from the balance differential, how the rank ladder is built, why energy is both a scalar number and a dynamics, and why measurement itself rides the same differential. It consolidates, it does not extend: every claim below is [structural/model-internal] unless marked otherwise, per the honesty triad and the Two-Realm Register ([[Two-Realm-Register]]).

## 1. Emergence — the registered doctrine

Emergence in LIMEN is **not** a substance appearing; it is a **registry event**: the overflow ([[A2-Overflow-From-Perfect-Symmetry]]) simultaneously creates (i) the silent boundary ([[A1-Silent-Boundary]]) and (ii) a **balance differential** — a difference of level between what remained the initial reference and what now has potential. Everything "existing" post-boundary is patterned on this differential; nothing is patterned on the pre-boundary itself, which stays silent (S1/S2 — revere).

**Registered stance:** emergence is only ever *in-model* emergence. What nature actually does with its own differentials is F3 — revere until aligned data. This reference makes no empirical cosmological claim.

## 2. The seed — ΔB, the balance differential

From [[A2-Overflow-From-Perfect-Symmetry]] (verbatim contract):

$$\Delta\mathcal{B} \equiv \text{initial-reference level} - \text{post-boundary potential level} \quad [\text{closure constant}]$$

- ΔB is a **rank-0 quantity**: a single number with no internal structure and no direction — the *purest possible* first distinction. It is the only thing the overflow needs to deposit; every richer structure downstream is **composition of this differential**, not an additional ingredient.
- Direction is not in ΔB. Direction appears at rank 1. This separation is load-bearing: it is why the onset arrow ([[Onset-Arrow]]) can be *created* by the overflow rather than pre-written in a pre-boundary law (T2 battery — passed).

The cascade from the differential to full dynamics. Each rank consumes the previous one; no rank introduces new substance.

**Reading rule (registered):** the ladder is *compositional*, not emergent-mystical. Rank n+1 adds zero substance; it adds **organization** of rank-n differentials. This is why the corpus can stay falsification-friendly: every rank's claim is a claim about organization, testable by the batteries.

## 4. Energy — the fundamental expression, both scalar and dynamic

The corpus registers energy as a **relative bookkeeping** quantity (never absolute — S4, revere):

1. **As a scalar number:** energy is the *ledger* of the balance differential — the numeric record of how much differential remains unexpressed at a site. A site's energy is a rank-0 fact about its registry state. [structural]
2. **As a dynamics:** energy-flow is the *stepping* of the same differential — constraint release (K2/T7), harmonic exchange (cavity ω₁ operators), lattice kinetics. The dynamics is not powered *by* energy; **the dynamics and the energy-ledger are two descriptions of one differential being spent**. [model-internal]

**Consequence (registered, fundamental-acceptance clause):** accepting ΔB as the fundamental deposit **necessarily** yields energy as scalar *and* dynamics — there is no third bookkeeping to invent. This is why the corpus never introduces a separate "energy substance": it would double-count the differential.

## 5. Measurement — the self-referential approach

Sensing and observing are performed **by the same differential**, not by an outside agent:

- A measurement event is a constraint-coupling between registers; the coupling itself is differential-driven (the same rank ladder, run inside the measuring apparatus).
- Therefore every measurement is **self-referential at the level of substrate**: the instrument is made of the same stepped differentials it reports on. In-model, this is not a paradox — it is why Ward invariance ([[Two-Realm-Register]] W7 · [[Aligned-Protocol]] §5) matters: outputs must be invariant under narrative decoration precisely because *measurement cannot import content from outside the differential*.
- **Honest limit:** the corpus has no independent-operator evidence that natural measurement behaves this way — the claim is registered as model-internal method (the aligned-operator discipline), flagged F3-adjacent. It prescribes *how we measure in-model*; it does not assert *how nature measures itself*.
