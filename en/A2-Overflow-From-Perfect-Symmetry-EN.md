# A2 — Overflow From Perfect Symmetry

## Axiom 2 — Constraint overflow from what was bound to perfect symmetry

> **Structural Causal Chain (LIMEN):**
> perfect symmetry (timeless, directionless) → constraint accumulation → overflow point → **the boundary** (silence) + spill into the posterior

Perfect symmetry means there is no distinction anywhere for "motion" to be made of — yet the constraint of level-matching itself has content: preserving every possible distribution is itself a constraint. The narrative says: accumulated constraint in the perfectly symmetric state overflows at some point; the overflow is the **first phenomenon** (not the outcome of an instability in time — time does not exist yet). The overflow creates two things at once: the **silent boundary** (A1) and a **level-balance deficit** between what remains the primordial reference and what now carries potential.

## Form — the balance deficit and potential

The balance deficit is taken to be a fixed scalar, not a field:

$$\Delta\mathcal{B} \equiv \text{primordial reference level} - \text{posterior potential-bearing level} \quad [\text{closure constant}]$$

The posterior potential is inherently **directed**: a forward arrow (A3). The overflow *creates* this arrow; it is not written into the prior law.

## Epistemic Status

This axiom is a **structural-analogical proposition**: it has no direct testability (the pre-boundary is unspeakable); but it has two in-model consequences that were tested: (1) the overflow is a finite event, not a continuous leak (T1 — silence after the quench); (2) the arrow appears without being placed in the law (T2). Any further exercise of this axiom is a claim of metaphysics, not physics.
